# FOR-024 — Controlled Apple Fixture Governance

The future fixture must originate from a lawfully owner-controlled test device,
controlled test accounts, and purpose-created synthetic contacts,
communications, calls, and attachments. Client, litigation, employee, family,
production credential, uncontrolled cloud, and third-party personal data are
prohibited.

The first matrix is one unencrypted Apple local backup created with Apple
Devices for Windows. Exact device model, iOS version/build, host OS, Apple
Devices version, timezone, operator, and start/end times must be recorded but
may not be generalized. Candidate events cover SMS, iMessage, threads,
participants, one-to-one and practical group conversations, attachments,
incoming/outgoing/missed calls, contacts, and backup/device metadata.

SHA-256 records byte consistency only, never Apple authenticity or support.
Custody is controlled test-fixture handling, not a claim of legally sufficient
chain of custody. The raw package stays encrypted at rest, access controlled,
outside Git/CI/email/public links/consumer sharing/LLM upload, and inside the
separately approved validation environment. Repository records may contain only
package identity, nonsensitive metadata, hashes, profile results, summaries,
and limitations.
